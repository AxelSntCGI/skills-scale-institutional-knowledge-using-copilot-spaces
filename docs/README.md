# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Quick Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. Our framework guides teams through five key lifecycle stages: **Initiation** (defining problems and stakeholders), **Planning** (breaking work into shippable increments), **Execution** (building, testing, and iterating), **Release** (deploying and verifying), and **Close & Retrospective** (capturing learnings).

Our project management model is built on core principles of customer-first delivery, psychological safety, and transparent communication. Each project has defined roles including Project Managers (coordinating delivery and communications), Product Managers (defining outcomes and measuring success), Developers (implementing features and collaborating on design), QA/Testing (validating quality), and Stakeholders (providing inputs and approvals). We maintain consistent communication through weekly syncs between PM and PdM, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed.

Key to our success is our commitment to quality and risk management. We employ clear acceptance criteria, automated testing in CI/CD pipelines, regular demos and reviews, and structured escalation paths for blockers. Our risk management process includes maintaining a risk register, identifying dependencies early, and communicating status transparently across all stakeholder groups.

## Key Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named Project Manager and Product Lead roles
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning across all teams
